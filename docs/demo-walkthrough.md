# Demonstrate the platform in 5–10 minutes

## Before the meeting

Run npm install, npm run seed, npm run dev. Open http://localhost:5173. Leave the terminal running. The preview banner should say January 1, 2027. Seven first-week readings are published; 358 imported drafts are available to the author for review.

Use Chrome device emulation at 390 × 844 for the mobile demonstration. Browser installation is optional and depends on browser support; network/API access is still required for reading.

## 0:00–1:30 — The book, brought online

Show the actual cover on the homepage, the author's supplied biography artwork, the monthly theme guide, and the warm reading-oriented layout. Explain that the date banner previews the 2027 book; it is not pretending today's date is January 2027.

Choose Explore a free reading. January 1, “THANK GOD, WE MADE IT.”, is the author’s actual text from PDF page 5.

## 1:30–3:30 — A reader's daily journey

Go to Sign in and select Subscriber under Explore the local demo. Show Grace's greeting, featured devotional, progress, journal and membership.

Choose Pause & read. Show Bible text, memory verse, reflection, Think About This, prayer, and daily challenge. Mark completed, save the reading, write a personal reflection, then return to the dashboard to show progress. Open My Growth Journal to show the saved reflection. It is private to this reader.

Visit the library; search “WHO AM I” and filter January. Explain that members can read the whole published archive regardless of the preview date.

## 3:30–5:00 — Membership without real charges

Open /login and select Guest, or register a new demonstration account. January 1 is accessible; premium readings require membership.

Open Membership/pricing, point out the proposed prices, choose Monthly and show the Demo Payment label. Select manual renewal and Simulate payment. No card details or real funds are involved.

Show payment history, cancel future renewal, and point out that access remains until the paid period ends. Manual renewal extends access. Automatic renewal is a preference only.

## 5:00–7:30 — The author's desk

Open /login, choose Admin, then visit /admin. Show metrics, subscriber list, engagement and audit activity.

Open Devotionals, edit an imported draft, and show all the editable sections. Review it against the private source before publishing. You can save an existing entry unchanged to demonstrate persistence without altering the author’s writing.

Show Plans for editable proposed pricing, Payments for simulated records, Inquiries for hardcover purchase interest, and Settings for the preview date.

## 7:30–9:00 — Mobile and hardcover

Switch to the mobile viewport. Show bottom navigation and comfortable reading typography. On Bookstore, demonstrate the hardcover inquiry form; explain that NGN 5,000 is the listed hardcover price, while stock, fulfillment and payment are not live.

## Close honestly

The first seven readings have been visually verified. All 365 were structurally extracted, but 358 remain drafts, including a flagged February 13 source anomaly. Author review, real payments, email, production operations, audio and native apps are later phases. Nothing has been deployed.
